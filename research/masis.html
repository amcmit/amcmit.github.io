MASIS

Multi-Agent Supplier Intelligence System

A five-stage agentic pipeline — Plan, Route, Retrieve, Verify, Synthesize — that orchestrates vector, relational, and graph databases behind a single natural-language interface, so procurement teams can ask cross-source questions that used to require three separate tools and manual reconciliation.

Explain it:

The problem

A procurement team's knowledge is scattered: contracts in one system, spend in another, supplier relationships in a third. Answering one real question — "who are we single-sourced with and out of contract?" — means logging into several tools and stitching the answers together by hand.

MASIS lets you ask in plain English and does the stitching for you — and it can show its work.

MASIS orchestrates six databases across three modalities (vector, SQL, graph) through a five-stage agentic pipeline. A hybrid 3-tier router — LLM reasoning → embedding similarity → domain heuristics — decides which sources a question needs, and bounded verification loops gate the answer on correctness and provenance before synthesis.

It serves both human users and other AI agents, and consolidates years of prior in-house research (spend categorization, text-to-SQL, RFx retrieval) into one orchestrated layer — benchmarked across five enterprise retrieval architectures on 80 curated queries.

Why it matters

  • One natural-language interface over vector, relational, and graph sources — no tool-switching or manual reconciliation.
  • Bounded verification gates answers on correctness and provenance, which is essential for high-stakes procurement decisions.
  • Serves both people and other agents, turning siloed prior work into one reusable intelligence layer.
